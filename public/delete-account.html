ULearn School — Delete Your Account & Data

Last updated: 12 June 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your ULearn School student-app account and the personal data associated with it. It applies to the ULearn student app published by ULearn English School, Dublin, Ireland.

How to request deletion

  1. Email app@ulearnschool.com from the email address you use to sign in to the app.
  2. Put "Delete my account and data" in the subject line.
  3. We will verify your identity, delete your app account, and confirm by email.

You can send this request at any time. We action verified requests promptly and within 30 days at the latest.

What is deleted

Your app sign-in account (email and password), your profile information, in-app messages and support requests, and documents you uploaded through the app (for example passport or IRP images).

What is kept, and for how long

As a registered language school in Ireland, ULearn is legally required to retain certain enrolment, attendance, payment and immigration-compliance records for the periods set by Irish education and immigration law, even after your app account is deleted. These records are retained only for those legal obligations and are not used to provide the app once your account is removed. After the legal retention period expires, this data is deleted.

Do students have to open a bank account?

The vast majority of ULearn students do not have to open a bank account while studying with us. 

Non-EU students who obtain a visa/permission to stay in Ireland for 8 months are required to open a bank account so they can show their finances to immigration authorities. 

EU students may open bank accounts in Ireland if they find a job and want to stay here for a long time.

How do students open a bank account?

ULearn can help you open a account with Cloud Payments. This is a cloud based banking system which provides students with an ATM/debit card and all the normal functions of a bank (statements etc). It is important to note that it is not a "regular" bank with locations students can visit therefore students cannot lodge cash/cheques and cannot speak to people face-to-face.

To open an account students need to show the following documents:
• Copy of the photo page of their passport - must be clear and visible. 
• Evidence of their address in Ireland*
• School Letter from ULearn


*Banks have very specific requirements about what documents they will accept as proof of address. You must provide one of the following:

• an insurance certificate with the student's name and Irish address printed on it
• a letter from the student's bank in their home country, sent to the student at their Dublin address
• a utility bill (i.e. electricity or gas) addressed to the student at their Irish address.

Where can students open the account? Can students open a Student Account?

Students can come to the reception or they can email ULearn and we will communicate with the bank on students' behalf.

The bank will send all documents / cards etc to the school (unless students ask us to send them elsewhere). 

The process normally takes 7 - 14 days.

At the time of writing (Oct 2018), the accounts which ULearn can open for students are regular accounts (i.e. they are not student accounts) so students have pay fees and charges.

This means there is a cost for having the account and for every transaction made with the bank card (taking money from an ATM, buying something in a shop). We hope in the future to be able to offer student accounts which smller charges. In the event we can offer this, students will be the first to know.

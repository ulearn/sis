Can EU/EEA citizens work in Ireland?

Yes. There is absolutely no problem with EU citizens obtaining employment here in Ireland. EU students can stay in Ireland for as long or as short as they wish. 

Do EU/EEA students need any documentation?

In order to find a job, there is no need to have any documentation. Once a student has found a job they will  need to get a get a PPS number (see final paragraph below) and open a bank account (see Opening a Bank Account Section later in this Handbook)

Can non-EU/EEA citizens work in Ireland?

If here in Ireland on a 90-day visa, that non-EU/non-EEA student may not work .

If here in Ireland on a Working Holiday visa or completing an 8-month study/work visa, that non-EU/non-EEA student can work .

How many hours can a non-EU/EEA student work?

Non-EU/non-EEA students may work 20-hours per week at all times of the year.

In certain periods, Non-EU/non-EEA students may work up to 40-hours per week. Those periods are summer time (May - August) and December 15 to January 15.

If a student finds a job, they will need a PPS number (see below) and a bank account (see the Opening a Bank Account section later in this Handbook). 

What is a PPS number?

A PPS number is a Personal Public Service number. All employers and employees in Ireland have to have one. A PPS number is a unique reference number that helps people access social welfare benefits, public services and information in Ireland.

How can students get one?

To get a PPS number, students must first have a job or job offer.

When that happens, the student will need:

• A letter from an employer confirming employment or offering a job, 
• Passport or national identity card, and
• Evidence of address (household bill, bank statement, health insurance). Please note that a ULearn school letter is not sufficient as evidence of address.

Students have to take all these documents to the PPS Office, 17 King’s Inn Street, Dublin Once there, they can fill in the PPS application form and hand it in with the documents. The number/card will be posted to the address on the documents handed in.

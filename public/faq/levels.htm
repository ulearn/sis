How does ULearn decide which level students start in?

After a student buys an English course at ULearn, they receive an email which contains a link to ULearn's Online Placement Test. The answers a student gives to the test gives us a good idea of their level of English.

When students arrive on their first day, they complete a speaking assessment with the Director of Studies or with a secior member of the academic staff. We then check students' speaking level with the level in their Online Test and we place them in the most appropriate level group.

Can students move to a higher level if they think the classes are too easy?

Yes, they can. If a student finds their class is too easy, they can speak to their teacher or to the Director of Studies.

Every Friday, every ULearn class does a test on the material swhich has been studied that week. These tests give the school a clear picture of every student's progress. If a student wants to move, the teacher and the Director of Studies assess the student's progress and participation in the group first. If there is any uncertainty, the Director of Studies may ask the student to do another test (either written or speaking) and, depending, on the result of that test, the student may be moved up to the next level.

It is very important to be patient. Learning a language takes time. We monitor every students' progress to make sure they improve but we also want to maintain the integrity of every class level. Therefore, we move students to the next level as soon as their level is good enough.

Can students move to a lower level if they think the classes are too difficult?

Yes, of course. As we say above, we do our very best to put students in the correct level at the beginning of their course but sometimes students may end up in a class that is above their level. Some students, for example, may be able to speak English much better than they can write it. Thei grammar may not be as good as their vocabulary. There can be many reasons why a student might feel a lower level would be better for them.

If this happens, the learner is welcome to speak to their teacher or to the Director of Studies. Again, we may ask the student to do another test (spoken or written) but if it is correct to move that student, then we will do so.

How long do students have to spend in one level before they can move up?

Unfortunately, there is no definite answer for this question. It depends on a lot of things and there is no one answer that fits all students. Having said that, most students will spend an average amount of time to pass between each level. You can see these times below:

A1: 8 – 16 weeks (12 weeks)
A2: 8 – 16 weeks (12 weeks)
B1: 12 – 24 weeks (16-20 weeks)
B2: 16 – 24 weeks (20 weeks)
C1: 24 – 32 weeks (28 weeks)

So, if ULearn places a student in an A1 group when they start their course, that learner might spend 6 weeks there (if they were a strong A1 English user already) but that student could spend 12 weeks or more there if their English was weak to begin with. And when that student moves to A2, they will probably spend minimum 12 weeks there because they have joined the A2 level as a low A2 English user.

It is very important to be patient. Language learning needs time. ULearn monitors every students' progress to make sure they are improving. We will move students to the next level as soon as their English level is good enough.

Attendance

ULearn wants all students to enjoy themselves and get the maximum value for their money. The best way to do this is by coming to class on time and staying until the end but we know there are days when people have to arrive late or leave early and we do our best to be understanding of this.

How is student's attendance recorded?

Every day students must sign in when they arrive for class and they must sign out at the end of class.

If a student arrives more than 15 minutes late for class, they may not sign the attendance sheet. Instead, the teacher will record the time the student has entered the class. Similarly, if the student leaves class early, they cannot sign the attendance sheet. They teacher will record the time they left the class.

What should students do if they can't come to school?

If a student cannot come to class for any reason, they should send an email to the school (info@ulearnschool.com) to tell the school they are not coming and to explain why.

If a student is absent, the teacher reports this to the school administration who may contact the student to see where they are. This is for students' own security - especially in the case of students who are under 18 years old.

Please look at the Email Templates section at the back of this Handbook for an example of the type of email students can send if they cannot come to class.

What happens if my attendance falls below 80%?

For EU students, nothing happens. There is no onus on students from EU countries (or indeed non-EU students in Ireland on a holiday visa) to come to class.

For long term (8 month) non-EU students this is not the case. Non-EU students must maintain a minimum attendance level of 80% at all times during their course as part of their permission to be in the country. If your attendance record is affected by late arrival or early departure from class, you will be asked to attend a meeting with the Director of Studies to explain why you’re arriving late or leaving early.

If your attendance record is affected by absence, you will receive an email if you do not come to class for 3 days in a row and you will be asked to attend a meeting with the Director of Studies to explain your absence.

Attendance is very important for non-EU students because they may not be given permission to stay in the country (or renew their visa) if they do not have the required attendance.

If a non-EU student misses a class, we send an email to remind that student about the importance of their attendance. In the case of repeated lateness, early departures or absence, the student will have to meet with the Director of Studies to explain their situation and resolve to attend class. 

In cases where the student shows no improvement, ULearn School reserves the right to suspend and potentially expel that student from courses with no refund of their fees.

ULearn provides students with a handy excel Attendance Calculator so students can calculate their own attendance relatively easily. Students can also request a correct attendance from the school durnig their course. 

ULearn Obligations to Immigration

ULearn is obliged to regularly inform Irish immigration authorities about non-EU students' attendance in and absences from classes. Failure to attend class or to adhere to the ULearn timetable may result in action being taken by ULearn or by the Irish immigration authorities.

Teachers & Class Management

We ask that students consider the teacher and their classmates. Our teachers are trying to manage classes and students coming and going at different times can make that job very difficult. It also has an impact on the enjoyment other students get from the class. Therefore, students who repeatedly arrive late for class will as a minimum be asked to wait until the break time before joining their group. In case of repeated lateness, the student may be asked to sit with our Director of Studies to explain themselves and explain how the situation will be resolved.

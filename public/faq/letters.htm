IMPORTANT INFORMATION ABOUT STUDENT LETTERS

Requests for student letters must be sent to the school by email to info@ulearnschool.com. You can click here to send us an e-mail now

ULearn will send the requested letter to the student within 48 hours.

Most documents will also be made available to the student within their app so students can download and print documents out at their convenience.

A printing service is available in ULearn for a small fee. Just email your document to 
ulearnprintjobs@hpeprint.com. All documents can be collected from the shop in the basement of the school building (13 Harcourt Street) during the break times. Black & white printing costs €0.10. Colour printing costs €0.30

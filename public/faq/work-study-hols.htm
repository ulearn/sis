As part of their permit to study in Ireland long term, non-EU students may take holidays from their course for 1 week, 2 weeks, 3 weeks or longer. Short-term students and EU students are not entitled to take breaks from their course.

According to INIS regulations, students should inform the school of any planned holidays when they're starting their course. It's not normally possible for ULearn to confirm course holidays requested after a student's course has begun.

Course holidays cannot be be longer than 33% of a student's completed course weeks. For example, if a student wishes to take holidays for a month (4 weeks), they must first complete 12 weeks of their course because 4 is 33% of 12.

Please follow these steps to request holidays from your course:



1. Before starting your course, send an email to info@ulearnschool.com. Please write: “Holiday Request” in the subject box. Students can use one of the texts in the Sample Email section in this app.

3. In the email, please clearly state the start and end dates of your course holidays. Remember that all holidays must be for full weeks (starting on a Monday and ending on a Friday). Taking days off is not permitted. 

4. Expect a response/confirmation email from the school within 24 hours. If no confirmation email is received from ULearn, the holiday request may not have been received. The student should contact the school again to confirm.

5. Students should not travel outside the country until they've registered with INIS and received their IRP.

Where can students find out about the week’s school activities?

ULearn organises social activities every week. Students can find out what activities are happening in the school every week by looking at the noticeboard in their class, at the noticeboard in the entrance hall or by checking our Facebook page

Are the school activities free?

Usually. Visits to museums and galleries, greyhound racing, cinema nights, and pub crawls are some of the events that we line up for our students. Most of them are for free, but if students have to pay, we do our very best to secure a student or group rate.

Are there activities at the weekends?

Yes, there are. We go on day trips to the main tourist attractions in Ireland, such as The Cliffs of Moher, The Giant’s Causeway, Belfast and Connemara.

Can students bring their friends to events?

Absolutely! Friends and family of students are more than welcome! All these activities are open to past, present and future students as well.

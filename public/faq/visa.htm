The Irish government grants students a visa to study English at ULearn for 25 weeks. As part of this visa programme, the Irish government permits students to work while they are studying.

This visa comes with conditions. It is very important for students to know and understand these conditions. If students do not meet these conditions, their visa could be revoked.

Here are the conditions:

1. Students are enrolled in a 25-week full-time English course at ULearn English School. If this is the student's first course, they cannot change courses or go to another language school until they have completed the 25 weeks.

2. The full-time course consists of 15 hours per week - 3 hours every day. Students' attendance must be higher than 75% after the first 6 weeks of their course and 85% or higher at the end of their course.

3. Students must sit an externally assessed English exam such as a Cambridge ESOL, IELTS or TIE (Test of Interactive English) exam when they have completed their 25-week course.

4. Students may work a maximum of 20 hours a week from mid-January to April and from September to mid-December.

5. Students may work a maximum of 40 hours a week between May and August and between December 15 and January 15.

6. Students may only take holidays from their course after they have completed 8 weeks.

7. Students must register with INIS for their Irish Residence Permit (IRP) as soon as possible after they arrive in Dublin. Appointments can be made at the following URL: https://burghquayregistrationoffice.inis.gov.ie/

8. Students must carry their IRP ID card at all times.

9. Students must obey all the laws of the Irish Republic while they are in Ireland.

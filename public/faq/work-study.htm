Do STUDY/WORK visa students really have to come to class every day? 

Yes, students do have to come to class every day! One of the most important conditions of the visa is that students attend their classes.

Students receive permission from the Irish government to come and live in Ireland for 8 months so they can improve their English. They are required to attend full-time English classes for 25 weeks.

Ok, so what are "full-time English classes?

Full-time classes consist of at least 15 hours of classes per week. Full-time English classes at ULearn take place in the morning from 9.00 to 12.20, Monday through Friday, and in the afternoon from 13.45 to 17.00, Monday to Friday.

If students don't come, will anybody know? 

Yes, we will know. ULearn records the attendance of all students in all classes at the beginning and the end of every class. When students arrive late or leave early, the teacher records it on the class attendance sheet next to the student's name.

If a student finds a job, can they arrive to class late or leave early? 

No, this is not ok and is a problem for the student and for ULearn. The student has been given a STUDY-WORK visa, not a WORK-STUDY visa. Classes must take priority over work.

We can help students prepare a CV and we can give you tips how and where to find a job in Dublin, but students must find work that allow them to attend their classes without interruption. Students can only receive 100% daily attendance if they arrive on time and do not leave until it has finished (12.45 - morning classes or 17.00 - afternoon classes).

If a student occasionally arrives a little bit late for class because of traffic or transport delays, their attendance will not be affected.

If a student regularly arrives late for class, their attendance (for the days they arrive late) will be recorded at 50%.

Students arriving more than 15 minutes late for class will have their attendance recorded at 0%.

Students leaving a class before it finishes will have their attendance for that day recorded at 0%.

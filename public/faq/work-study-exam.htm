Why do non-EU visa students have to do a test at the end of their course?

There are two reasons long-term non-EU students have to do an exam.

Firstly, because it is a condition of their visa/permission to remain in Ireland that they undertake an exam. Secondly, because those students have attended full-time English classes for 25 weeks it is important for them to check their level of English at the end of their course and understand what they have achieved after all their work.

But students do progress tests every week during their course and some get good scores all the time.

ULearn progress tests are a different type of test. We use progress tests to give learners the opportunity to interact with the language they’ve been using in class during that week and to assess their ability to use it.  It is helpful for us to monitor progress but also for students to refresh their memory on things they learned during the week.

The end of course test is a what we call a proficiency test. It assesses students ability to use English in general.

Where can students do this proficiency test?

Students have different options depending on the test they choose to do.

If, for example, the student wants to do a Cambridge ESOL test like the PET or the First Certificate, they have to go to a Cambridge Examination Centre and do the test there.

If the student wants to do the Test of Interactive English (TIE), ULearn can arrange for them to take the test here in ULearn.

What's the difference between a Cambridge ESOL test and a TIE test?

Cambridge ESOL tests are prepared by Cambridge ESOL and are recognized globally as reliable indicators of a person’s English proficiency. Cambridge ESOL tests measure the student's ability with the four skills of speaking, listening, reading and writing.

TIE tests are prepared by an Irish company called IELT (Interactive English Language Tests). IELT is a member of ELTA, the European Language Testing Association, and its tests are approved by Quality and Qualifications Ireland (QQI), the organisation that maintains international education standards in Irish educational centres. TIE tests measure your ability with spoken and written English.

Do students have to pay to do these tests?

Yes. Students have to pay ULearn when they start their course. The payment needs to be shown on the confirmation letter which is shown to the immigration authorities when the students goes to get their visa.
At the time of writing (October 2017) the Cambridge test costs approxaimtely €180 while the TIE test costs €100.

How can students prepare for the test they will do?

The ULearn syllabus includes exam preparation so students will already be familiar with the questions in both tests when they complete their course. Students can get extra help preparing for their exam from the Director of Studies.

Will ULearn book the test or do students have to book it themselves?

ULearn will book the test for students. The Director of Studies contacts students in the final month of their course to make the booking and check that the student is prepared.

Where can students find a job?

We recommend the following websites for students who would like to find work in Ireland:
• http://www.jobs.ie/
• http://www.irishjobs.ie/
• http://www.findajob.ie/
• http://www.gumtree.ie/s-jobs/v1c8p1
• http://www.monster.ie/
• http://www.toplanguagejobs.ie/
• http://www.adverts.ie/jobs/

On most of these sites, students can submit a copy of their curriculum vitae (CV) and apply on-line.

Unless the company asks you to apply on a website, we always recommend students visit the business and hand their CV directly to the manager.

Sometimes, students can be in the right place at the right time and a quick conversation might bring success! At the very least, it gives the responsible person a chance to meet the student and listen to their English. Students should also remember to ask the manager's name so they ask for that person if they go back later.

Can ULearn help students with their CVs?

Unfortunately, it is not possible for ULearn to correct all our students' CVs individually so we offer a CV workshop every couple of weeks which is like a class where students bring their CV and then ask the teacher for help and advice.

Where should students start with their CV. How should they begin?

Send an email to info@ulearnschool with "CV Template" as the subject we will send a CV template. This is what a CV in Ireland should look like. Be careful because some things students write on CVs in other countries are not normal here and vice versa.

See the email templates section at the back of this handbook for assistance how to write to us.

What should a student do when they enter all their info into the CV template?

Save it and bring it to the CV Workshop class. Students can either bring it in paper form or on their laptop. It is better to bring it on a computer because changes can be recorded and different versions can be saved.

Students can sign up to the CV Workshop class at the noticeboard in the reception area.

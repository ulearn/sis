The following emails are examples of the type of communication students should try to write - either when communicating with ULearn or with other people in English. Please remember to fill in the fields in brackets () with information, such as the name of the person receiving the mail, your name, dates etc

#1 SAMPLE - HOLIDAY REQUEST EMAIL (A) - (LONG-TERM STUDENTS ONLY)

Hi Antonella,

I’m writing to request holidays starting on Monday, February 26 and ending on Friday, March 15.

Best,
(your name)


#2 SAMPLE - HOLIDAY REQUEST EMAIL (B) - (LONG-TERM STUDENTS ONLY)

Hi Antonella,
 
I’m planning to go travelling in February next year, so I'd like to request holidays starting on Monday, February 12 and ending on Friday, February 16.

Regards,
(your name)

#3 SAMPLE - ABSENCE EMAIL (A) - (ALL STUDENTS)

Hi Antonella

I’m writing to let you know that I can’t come to class today because (insert your reason here). I expect to be back in class (day you will come back).

Regards,
(your name)

#4 SAMPLE - ABSENCE EMAIL (B) - ALL STUDENTS

Hi Antonella,

I’m just writing to tell you that I’m not coming to class tomorrow because I have to (insert reason here). I’ll be back in class on (day you will come back).

Best,
(your name)

#5 SAMPLE - STUDENT TRAVEL CARD EMAIL (only necessary for students aged 24+)

Hi Antonella,

I’d like to get a student travel card so I’d be grateful if you could prepare a school letter for me.

My address is as follows:
(insert your addresss here: 123 Street Name, Area Name, Postcode)

Thanks for your help.

Regards,
(your name)

#6 SAMPLE - BANK ACCOUNT EMAIL

Hi Antonella,

I’d like to open a bank account. Could ULearn please help me to do this?

My address is as follows:
(insert your addresss here: 123 Street Name, Area Name, Eircode)

Thanks for your help.

Regards,
(your name)

Best

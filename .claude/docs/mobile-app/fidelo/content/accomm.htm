What Accommodation can ULearn organise?

ULearn offers two types of accommodation for students.

Apartments

ULearn apartments are located in Dublin city centre approximately 20 - 30 minutes walk from ULearn. We have single rooms, large rooms with double beds, and large en suite rooms, and shared rooms for freidns travelling together so we can cater for all types of students.

The apartments are modern and clean and provide students with comfort and convenience. The apartments have shared cooking facilities, wi-fi, television and dvd, and are professionally cleaned each week. The apartments are only for ULearn customers so students can practice their English and make friends easily. 

Host Families

ULearn has a select network of Host Families located 20 - 30 minutes from ULearn on public transport. In Dublin, families do not live in the city centre so it would be unusual to find a family where students can walk to school. 

Host family accommodation is half-board which means students get breakfast and dinner if they want. Each home is different. Sometimes, the family is a large family with children, sometimes they are small but the one thing all our families have in common is that they make sure the students are well taken care of. They chat with students every day and make them feel at home. 

Can students organise their own accommodation?

Yes absolutely although finding accommodation in Dublin can be difficult. Therefore, we always recommend new students take 1 or 2 weeks accommodation with us. It is not a requirement though and if a student wants to organise their own accommodation they are welcome to do so.

Here are some useful sites for students looking to find accommodation in Dublin:
• www.daft.ie
• www.let.ie
• www.rent.ie
• www.gumtree.ie
• The Ideal Flatmate Dublin Group on www.facebook.com

Also, students can find accommodation through other students. Talk to classmates and see what they are doing and feel free to check the noticeboard beside the stairs in the school reception. Sometimes, students look for a flatmate or ask for a room by putting an ad up there.

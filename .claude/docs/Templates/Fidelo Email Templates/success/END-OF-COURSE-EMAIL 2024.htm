End-of-course exam

If you haven't spoken to or emailed Antonella or Paul to organize a date for your end-of-course exam, please do this immediately. Doing this exam is one of the conditions of your residence permit. You can email Antonella at success@ulearnschool.com or visit her in the reception area of the school. 

Exit letter

An exit letter is a document you receive from your school after you complete your course. You need this document if you want to renew your IRP. Your exit letter states your final course attendance percentage and the date of your end-of-course exam, so you must agree this date with us before we can provide you with your exit letter.

End-of-course exam certificate

If you did a Cambridge exam, the exam centre will send your certificate to ULearn and we'll contact you to let you know you can come and collect it. If you're leaving Ireland and want us to send you the certificate in the post, you should speak to Antonella in reception about arranging this. 

If you did an IELTS exam, the exam centre will send the test report form to the address you provided in your IELTS portal.

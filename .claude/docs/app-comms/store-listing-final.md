## App details
- **App name:** `ULearn School`
- **Short description (≤80):**
```
ULearn English School Dublin: your course, attendance, documents & classes.
```
- **Full description:**
```
The official student app for ULearn English School, Dublin.

ULearn is an accredited English-language school in the heart of Dublin. This app is your personal connection to the school — everything about your course, in one place, wherever you are.

Sign in with the details provided by the school to get started. The app is for enrolled ULearn students only.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Your course at a glance
See your enrolment details, course dates, level and weekly class timetable. Always know where you need to be and when.

• Track your attendance
Check your attendance record in real time and stay on top of the requirements for your visa and your studies. No surprises.

• Manage your documents
Upload and store your important documents — passport, IRP card and more — directly from your phone. Your enrolment and visa letters are kept safely in one place, ready whenever you need them.

• Book more courses
Continuing your studies or adding weeks? Request a new booking from inside the app and the school takes care of the rest.

• Message the school
Get in touch with the ULearn team whenever you need help — questions about your course, your accommodation, your documents or anything else. Support is only a tap away.

• Social calendar
Discover the activities, trips and events happening at the school and around Dublin, and make the most of your time in Ireland.

• Learning
Access your class materials and lessons to keep studying between sessions.

WHO IT'S FOR

This app is designed exclusively for current students of ULearn English School. You'll need an account created by the school and the email address you registered with. If you can't sign in, contact the school and we'll get you set up.

ABOUT ULEARN

ULearn English School delivers high-quality English courses to international students in Dublin, with a focus on real progress, friendly teaching and a great student experience. This app is part of the service we provide to make your time with us simple, organised and stress-free.

Questions or feedback? Email app@ulearnschool.com.
```
